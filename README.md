## Hamdan Ashfaq

PhD student in Computational Science at **UC Irvine** and **San Diego State University**. I build machine learning methods for materials characterization, mostly electron microscopy: finding hidden order in 4D-STEM diffraction data and classifying microstructures from SEM images.

Advised by Prof. Mehmet Dogan (SDSU) and Prof. Ruqian Wu (UCI). Based in San Diego.

### Research

**The Ohio State University, Center for Electron Microscopy and Analysis (CEMAS)** · Visiting Fellow, Hwang Lab
* 4D-STEM multislice simulations in collaboration with Sandia National Laboratories.
* Self-supervised learning on 4D-STEM nanodiffraction patterns of amorphous semiconductors (Intel-funded SURE fellowship). Poster at Microscopy & Microanalysis 2024.
* Co-author on a manuscript under review at *Acta Materialia*.

**Purdue University** · Summer Undergraduate Research Fellow, Chawla group
* ResNet-50 and U-Net++ models that classify Sn-58Bi solder microstructures from SEM images with 90.1% accuracy.

**Denison University** · Senior capstone
* Automated fold symmetry detection across 65,536 nanodiffraction patterns of CuZr metallic glass. Best Senior Capstone Project, Department of Data Analytics.
